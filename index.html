Dissertation · January 2022

Mobile Banking Adoption in India: Behaviour Reasoning Theory

  1. Question

    What drives people to adopt or resist mobile banking?

  2. Framework

    Behavioural Reasoning Theory

  3. Data

    162 current mobile-banking customers

  4. Method

    Quantitative analysis using multiple and linear regression

  5. Insight

    Convenience and ubiquity supported adoption; traditional barriers were a key reason against it.

  6. Learning

    Data becomes valuable when it explains behaviour and supports a better decision.

The sample focused on urban participants under 40; the findings should be read in that context.

What I gained
