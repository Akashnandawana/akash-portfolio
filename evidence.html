How I lead / Managing a BI team

Set clear outcomes.
Give people ownership.
Remove barriers.
Develop capability.

My role as a leader is not to manage every task. It is to create clarity, give people ownership, remove barriers and help them succeed.

01

Direction

  • Set annual SMART objectives.
  • Align individual objectives with company and departmental OKRs.
  • Define measurable outcomes and explain why the work matters.
02

Ownership

  • Give team members ownership of outcomes.
  • Encourage self-management and focus on outcomes rather than activity.
  • Give people room to determine how they will solve problems.
03

Coaching

  • Hold weekly 1-to-1 discussions to identify roadblocks.
  • Support difficult challenges and coach rather than micromanage.
  • Help team members make decisions independently.
04

Development & wellbeing

  • Hold monthly individual conversations to review the month.
  • Discuss performance and development; create a safe opportunity to discuss personal challenges that may require support.
  • Identify opportunities for development and increased responsibility.
I want my team to understand why the work matters, not simply what task they have been given.

Mobile Banking Adoption in India: Behaviour Reasoning Theory

  1. Question

    What drives people to adopt or resist mobile banking?

  2. Framework

    Behavioural Reasoning Theory

  3. Data

    162 current mobile-banking customers

  4. Method

    Quantitative analysis using multiple and linear regression

  5. Insight

    Convenience and ubiquity supported adoption; traditional barriers were a key reason against it.

  6. Learning

    Data becomes valuable when it explains behaviour and supports a better decision.

The sample focused on urban participants under 40; the findings should be read in that context.
